The left click that locks the pointer (`controls::grab_pointer`) is sometimes also seen by `targeting::edit_blocks` as a break click, so capturing the mouse breaks whatever the crosshair is on. Seen intermittently in headless drives (chest-1's CHEST stage, herd's re-run on af1c65a: total edits 1 where the stage made 0). menu-2 noted the ordering dependence earlier: Resume edits nothing only because `edit_blocks` runs before `grab_pointer`.

Fix the rule: a click that changes the lock state must not also act in the world (e.g. `edit_blocks` ignores buttons pressed in a frame where the pointer was unlocked at frame start, or a `JustLocked` marker), independent of system order. Related: mem-99d0 (left-button mouse lock, shorn).

Verify: a unit/app test that a lock click edits nothing regardless of system order; drive.js plain run and CHEST stage report 0 total edits across several runs.
