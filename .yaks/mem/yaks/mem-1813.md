The left click that locks the pointer (`controls::grab_pointer`) is sometimes also seen by `targeting::edit_blocks` as a break click, so capturing the mouse breaks whatever the crosshair is on. Seen intermittently in headless drives (chest-1's CHEST stage, herd's re-run on af1c65a: total edits 1 where the stage made 0). menu-2 noted the ordering dependence earlier: Resume edits nothing only because `edit_blocks` runs before `grab_pointer`.

Fix the rule: a click that changes the lock state must not also act in the world (e.g. `edit_blocks` ignores buttons pressed in a frame where the pointer was unlocked at frame start, or a `JustLocked` marker), independent of system order. Related: mem-99d0 (left-button mouse lock, shorn).

Verify: a unit/app test that a lock click edits nothing regardless of system order; drive.js plain run and CHEST stage report 0 total edits across several runs.

▸ 2026-11-02T06:51:58Z [lockfix-1]

Follow-up root cause (herd: 1 in 5 CHEST runs made 1 edit): caught it with step marks + temporary DIAG logging. The edit was 'broke chest' at the start of the stage, from the real scene lock click. The earlier second 'Controls' click is seen by Bevy only after the drive has already moved the cursor to SCENE (SwiftShader frames take >1 s), so grab_pointer took it for a scene click (on_button=false, pos=(200,360)) and set the lock intent. Browser grants the lock later; the real SCENE click then arrives with locked=true and platform=true and breaks the block under the crosshair. WorldMouse is correct here (the press did begin locked); the bug is the misread click that locked. Fix: grab_pointer treats a press as on a button if the cursor was over a button at the end of the previous frame as well (Local). New test in controls.rs; menu/picker tests now idle a frame off the button before the scene click. Next: rebuild web, 10+ clean CHEST runs, gates.

▸ 2026-11-02T07:53:58Z [lockfix-1]

Decision: grab_pointer keeps a Local 'over a button at the end of the previous frame' and treats a press as on a button if the cursor is over one now or was then. Rejected: reconstructing the press position from CursorMoved messages (no ordering across message types); a grace timer after a lock; blocking the real click when the lock was granted recently (hides the misread lock instead of preventing it). Cost: a real click within one frame of leaving a button is read as a button click and must be repeated (menu/picker tests now idle a frame off the button first).

Also: the old drive.js CHEST stage moved the pointer to the centre to undo a 440 px turn it attributed to the lock click. That turn was caused by the mistaken lock (look was active while moving to SCENE). With the lock fixed the move itself turned the view (chest never opened, 3 of 3 runs placed blocks), so the stage now clicks at SCENE like the LEVER stage.
