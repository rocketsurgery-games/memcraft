Decision: a door's open bit (4) is toggled on the LOWER half only, from a click on either half, not on both halves. Rejected: flipping bit 4 on both halves. Reason: `blocks::door` reads open from the lower and the hinge (bit 0) from the upper; a scan of the shipped world shows upper halves only as data 8/9 (hinge only; no open doors exist in the world to show otherwise), so bit 4 on an upper half would be a value the format does not have. Both halves still change on screen and in `Solids`, because the mesh/collision of each half is derived from the lower one. This reads the claim's "both halves of a door" as the outcome (the whole door opens), not as two data edits.

Decision: right click on an interactable block never places, even with a block selected (no sneak/shift override). Rejected: shift-to-place; the viewer has no sneak key.

Decision: a door whose other half is missing (lower broken/replaced) swallows the click and changes nothing, rather than placing a block against it. Rejected: returning None (the player would get a block placed against a door they tried to open).

Decision: gate open bit is a plain XOR of bit 4, with no push-direction logic (Java flips facing to the player). Rejected: orient by player position; placement/orientation is out of scope per the claim.
